# Email Templates: SCI Self-Certification Program

These are the standard email templates for program communications. Copy and fill in the bracketed placeholders for each use. All emails are sent from `sci-certification@greensoftware.foundation`.

---

## 1. Acknowledgement

Sent within 1 business day of receiving a submission.

---

**Subject:** SCI Self-Certification — Submission Received — [TRACKING-ID]

Dear [CONTACT-NAME],

Thank you for submitting your SCI self-certification application for **[SOFTWARE-NAME]** on behalf of **[ORGANIZATION-NAME]**.

Your submission has been assigned tracking number **[TRACKING-ID]** and has entered our review queue.

**What happens next:**

- A reviewer will evaluate your submission against our 7-item disclosure checklist within **10–15 business days**.
- If the reviewer recommends approval, the Software Standards Working Group has a 7-day sign-off period before your certificate is issued.
- Total time from submission to certificate is typically **3–4 weeks**.
- If anything in your submission needs attention, you'll receive specific feedback identifying exactly what to fix.

**Useful links:**

- [Applicant Guide](https://greensoftware.foundation/sci-certification/guide) — full requirements and evaluation criteria
- [Badge Usage Guidelines](https://greensoftware.foundation/sci-certification/badge-guidelines) — for after approval

If you have questions in the meantime, reply to this email.

Best regards,
GSF SCI Self-Certification Program

## 2. Revision Request

Sent when a reviewer marks one or more checklist items N or I.

---

**Subject:** SCI Self-Certification — Revision Requested — [TRACKING-ID]

Dear [CONTACT-NAME],

Thank you for your SCI self-certification submission for **[SOFTWARE-NAME]** ([TRACKING-ID]).

Your submission has been reviewed, and we need a few things to be revised before we can approve it. **This is not a rejection** — it is an invitation to address specific items and resubmit.

**Items that need attention:**

[PASTE REVIEWER FEEDBACK HERE — specific items by number with what needs to change. Example:]

- **Item 1 (Identity and scope):** Excluded components list generic rationales ("out of scope"). Each exclusion needs a system-specific rationale — e.g., "CDN is a separate service outside our operational boundary."
- **Item 4 (Energy and carbon intensity):** The energy breakdown lists values per component but does not identify data sources. Please state the data source for each component's energy figure.

**How to resubmit:**

Reply to this email with your updated submission, or send a new email to sci-certification@greensoftware.foundation with the subject line:

`SCI Self-Certification — Resubmission — [TRACKING-ID]`

You only need to resubmit the complete updated submission — no need to explain what changed separately. You may resubmit as many times as needed.

Your resubmission will enter the review queue and be reviewed within **10–15 business days**.

Best regards,
GSF SCI Self-Certification Program

## 4. Rejection

Sent only for bad faith submissions, after two reviewers concur. Never used for poor quality.

---

**Subject:** SCI Self-Certification — Submission Rejected — [TRACKING-ID]

Dear [CONTACT-NAME],

Your SCI self-certification submission for **[SOFTWARE-NAME]** ([TRACKING-ID]) has been reviewed by two independent reviewers, and regrettably your submission has been rejected.

**Reason for rejection:**

[PASTE SPECIFIC RATIONALE HERE — must include concrete evidence of bad faith. Example:]

Both reviewers independently determined that the submission contains fabricated data. Specifically: [describe the evidence].

Rejection is reserved for cases of bad faith and is never used for incomplete or low-quality submissions. This decision was made by two independent reviewers who each reviewed the submission separately.

**Your right to appeal:**

You may appeal this decision through our dispute resolution process:

1. **Informal resolution (days 1–10):** Reply to this email with a detailed rationale for why you believe the decision is incorrect. The Program Owner will attempt to resolve the matter directly.
2. **Formal review (days 11–30):** If unresolved, a 3-person panel from the review committee (excluding anyone involved in the original decision) will review written statements from both sides and issue a written decision.
3. **Final appeal (days 31–60):** If still unresolved, you may appeal to the SWG Chair. This appeal is limited to procedural errors, new evidence, or panel misconduct. The SWG Chair's decision is final.

To begin an appeal, reply to this email within 10 business days.

Best regards,
GSF SCI Self-Certification Program

## 5. Renewal Reminder

Sent 30 days before certificate expiry.

---

**Subject:** SCI Self-Certification — Renewal Reminder — [CERTIFICATE-ID] expires [EXPIRY-DATE]

Dear [CONTACT-NAME],

Your SCI self-certification for **[SOFTWARE-NAME]** ([CERTIFICATE-ID]) will expire on **[EXPIRY-DATE]** — that's 30 days from now.

**To renew:**

Submit a full new submission to sci-certification@greensoftware.foundation using the current submission template. Renewal goes through the same review process as a new submission.

- [Download the submission template](https://greensoftware.foundation/sci-certification/template)
- [Applicant Guide](https://greensoftware.foundation/sci-certification/guide)

Use the subject line: `SCI Self-Certification — Renewal — [CERTIFICATE-ID]`

**If you don't renew:**

Your certificate will be marked "Expired" on the expiry date. The certificate and your public disclosure will remain publicly visible as a historical record, but the badge should be removed from your materials once the certificate expires.

**What's changed since your last submission?**

If your SCI score has changed since your original submission, your renewal should reflect the updated measurement. If your methodology or boundary has changed, please describe what changed and why in Section 7 of the template.

If you have questions about the renewal process, reply to this email.

Best regards,
GSF SCI Self-Certification Program

## 7. Challenge Notification

Sent to a certificate holder when a community challenge is submitted against their certificate.

---

**Subject:** SCI Self-Certification — Community Challenge Received — [CERTIFICATE-ID]

Dear [CONTACT-NAME],

A community challenge has been submitted regarding your SCI self-certification for **[SOFTWARE-NAME]** ([CERTIFICATE-ID]).

**What this means:**

The SCI Self-Certification Program is based on self-certification with public disclosure. Any party may challenge a certificate if they believe the underlying calculation is non-conformant with ISO/IEC 21031:2024 or was made in bad faith. This is a normal part of the community validation process.

**Summary of the challenge:**

[PASTE SUMMARY OF CHALLENGE — what the challenger is disputing. Do not include the challenger's identity.]

**Your certificate status:**

Your certificate status has been changed to **"Under Community Review"** while this process is resolved.

**What you need to do:**

You have **30 days** from the date of this email to respond with clarifications, corrections, or a rebuttal. Please reply to this email with your response.

Your response should address the specific points raised in the challenge. You may provide additional evidence, correct your disclosure, or explain why you believe the challenge is unfounded.

**What happens next:**

1. GSF has reviewed the challenge and determined it is not frivolous (this step is already complete).
2. You respond within 30 days (this step).
3. GSF evaluates your response within 15 business days.
4. Outcome: challenge dismissed, minor corrections requested, certificate revoked, or bad faith determined.

If the challenge is found to be unfounded, your certificate returns to "Active" status with no changes. If minor issues are identified, you'll be asked to correct your disclosure and your certificate remains valid with a note: "Updated in response to community feedback."

For the full community challenge process, see the [Applicant Guide](https://greensoftware.foundation/sci-certification/guide).

Best regards,
GSF SCI Self-Certification Program

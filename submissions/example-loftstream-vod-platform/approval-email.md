# Example Approval Email: LoftStream Media Ltd — Origin & Player

> **This is an example approval email.** It demonstrates what the applicant
> receives after their submission passes the review checklist and a
> certificate is issued.

**From:** sci-certification@greensoftware.foundation
**To:** yuki.tanaka@loftstream.example.com
**Subject:** SCI Self-Certification Approved — LoftStream Media Ltd — Origin & Player — GSF-SCI-2026-00004

---

Dear Yuki,

Your SCI self-certification submission for **LoftStream Origin & Player v5.1.0** has been reviewed and **approved**.

**Certificate ID:** GSF-SCI-2026-00004
**Certificate:** https://badges.greensoftware.foundation/credentials/gsf-sci-2026-00004
**Public disclosure:** https://github.com/greensoftware-foundation/sci-certifications/blob/main/certifications/2026/GSF-SCI-2026-00004/disclosure.md
**Valid until:** 2027-06-04

Your submission has been published as your public disclosure (your contact email has been redacted). Badge assets (SVG, PNG, light and dark variants) are available from your certificate page.

Before using the badge, review the usage rules in the Applicant Guide — in particular, always include the "self-certified" qualifier when referencing ISO/IEC 21031:2024 conformity. As your boundary excludes the third-party CDN and viewer devices, please ensure marketing materials clarify that the certification covers the origin and metadata infrastructure and not end-to-end content delivery.

To renew, submit an updated SCI measurement before **2027-05-05**. We will send a reminder 30 days before expiry.

If you have any questions, reply to this email.

Congratulations,
